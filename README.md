## 🧪 Featured Projects

Two open-source AI tools for clinical trial teams. Both are free to try in the browser, with fictional data only.

### [SCOPE](https://github.com/FabcanK6/scope): Site Communication & Oversight Processing Engine

Reads clinical trial monitoring visit notes and returns a risk level, every finding with the sentence that shows it, action items and a draft follow-up letter.

- **The AI reads; plain code decides.** Every quote is checked against the note, deadlines are counted by code, and the risk level comes from a fixed rubric.
- Judges each visit against the study's own protocol, tracks sites across visits, and learns from rulings its users agree on.
- On a 25-note stress test (fictional notes), it caught all 10 high-risk visits and matched the expected risk level on 24.

**[▶ Try SCOPE live](https://scope-fabcank6.streamlit.app)** · [Code](https://github.com/FabcanK6/scope)

### [iVIS](https://github.com/FabcanK6/ivis): Intelligent Visualization Insight Synthesizer

Turns plain-English dashboard requests (*"top 10 sites with the most open queries in Germany, last 30 days"*) into Power BI visuals: chart type, fields, filters, a `visual.json` file and a work item.

- A fine-tuned BERT model plus keyword cues, or an AI reader whose answers are checked by code.
- Picks the right chart on 99.6% of 2,000 generated test requests (99.4% on phrasing it never saw in training).
- Bring your own data model: describe your measures and columns, and requests map onto your fields.

**[▶ Try iVIS live](https://ivis-fabcank6.streamlit.app)** · [Code](https://github.com/FabcanK6/ivis) · [Model on Hugging Face](https://huggingface.co/FabcanK6/ivis-bert)

Feedback is very welcome: open an issue on either repo or email me.
